--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What StudySync is

StudySync is a study collaboration app: accounts, classes, messaging, shared resources, and optional live video and audio sessions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Acceptable use and content

StudySync is for study and collaboration. You must not use the service to share, request, distribute, or solicit nudity, sexually explicit images or video, or other sexual content (including in chat, uploaded files, live sessions, or profile materials). We may remove violating content and suspend or terminate accounts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Information we process

• Account and profile: Information you provide when signing in (for example with Google) and profile details you add in the app.

• App content: Messages, class information, notes or files you upload, and similar content you choose to share in the service.

• Camera and microphone: Used when you join video or voice features in the app so you can be seen and heard in a session. We do not use the camera or microphone when you are not in a call or have not granted permission.

• Technical data: Device and connection data typical for mobile apps (for example to run the service and help keep it secure).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How we use it

We use this information to run StudySync: sign you in, sync your data, show classes and chats, enable calls, improve reliability, and keep the service secure.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Third-party services

The app may use providers such as:

• Google Firebase (for example sign-in and cloud database) — also covered by Google’s terms and privacy policy.

• LiveKit or a similar service for real-time video and audio — covered by that provider’s terms and policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Retention and deletion

We keep data as long as needed to provide the service or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your personal data where that applies — use the contact email above.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Account deletion (Google Play)

You can request deletion of your StudySync account and the personal data we associate with it.

1. Send an email to hlamulondhlovu11@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in to StudySync (or say clearly which account to delete).

2. Use the subject line: Delete my StudySync account.

3. We will confirm receipt and process your request within a reasonable time (typically within 30 days), unless we must retain certain information for legal or security reasons.

Deleting your account may remove or anonymize your profile and content you contributed, subject to backups, legal holds, and what other users or classes still need to function.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Children

The app is not directed at young children. Parents and guardians are responsible for use by minors. Make sure your settings in Google Play Console (target audience) match how you market the app.

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top.
