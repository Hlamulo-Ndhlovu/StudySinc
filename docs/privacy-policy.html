What StudySync is

StudySync is a study collaboration app: accounts, classes, messaging, shared resources, and optional live video/audio sessions.

StudySync is for study and collaboration. You must not use the service to share, request, distribute, or solicit nudity, sexually explicit images or video, or other sexual content (including in chat, uploaded files, live sessions, or profile materials). We may remove violating content and suspend or terminate accounts.

Information we process

  • Camera & microphone: Used when you join video or voice features in the app so you can be seen and heard in a session. We do not access the camera or microphone when you are not in a call or have not granted permission.

We use this information to run StudySync: authenticate you, sync your data, show classes and chats, enable calls, improve reliability, and keep the service secure.
