================================================================================
You need ONE public web link (https://...) for Google Play. Easiest path below.
================================================================================

Google Play does not host the policy for you. You paste a link into:
  Play Console → Policy / App content → Privacy policy

If you already use Play Console, you almost certainly have a Google account.
Use the SAME account for Google Sites (free). No coding required.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION A — Google Sites (recommended, ~10 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. On a computer, open:  https://sites.google.com/new

2. Sign in with your Google account if asked.

3. Click "Blank" (or any starter). Give the site a title at the top, e.g.:
      StudySync Privacy Policy

4. Click the page so you can type. Copy text from this folder:
      docs/privacy-policy-COPY-PASTE.txt
   Paste it into the page (you can paste everything at once, or section by section).

5. Find YOUR_EMAIL_HERE in the text and replace it with your real email.

6. Click "Publish" (top right).

7. Choose a short web address Google offers, e.g. studysync-privacy
   (You will get a link like: https://sites.google.com/view/studysync-privacy/home )

8. Click Publish again to confirm.

9. OPEN that link in a private/incognito window. You should see your policy
   without signing in. That is the link Google Play wants.

10. In Play Console → your app → App content → Privacy policy:
    Paste the full https:// link. Save.

11. In your Android project, open:
      app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml
    Set privacy_policy_url to that SAME https link (so Settings opens it).

12. Rebuild your release AAB and upload again to Play.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION B — You already use GitHub
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Create a new public repository (any name).

2. Upload docs/privacy-policy.html and rename it to index.html at the repo root,
   OR use GitHub's "Add file" and paste the HTML contents.

3. Enable GitHub Pages: Repository → Settings → Pages → Source: main branch,
   folder / (root). Save.

4. After a minute, GitHub shows a URL like:
      https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/YOUR_REPO/

5. Use that URL in Play Console (Privacy policy) and in strings.xml
   (privacy_policy_url).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the link works
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• In Play Console, complete "Data safety" so it matches what the policy says
  (camera, microphone, account, messages, etc.).

• You do NOT need to remove CAMERA from the app if the policy explains it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stuck?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• The link must start with https://
• The page must open for strangers (not "only people in my organization")
• Google Sites: when publishing, avoid restricting to "only people @yourdomain"
  unless that still allows the public (Play needs a normal public URL)
